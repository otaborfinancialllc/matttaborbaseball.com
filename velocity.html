The programming

What we actually do.

Every day has one job. I do not coach positions, I coach energy flow, so the same principles get applied differently to each athlete.

Days built around intent

Light days, build days, velo days. You know how hard today is supposed to be before you pick up a ball, and each one is laid out in the PitchSafe app.

Throws that build the move

Holds, then rocker, step-behind and walking throws, plus plyos at 60, 80 and 100%. Each series gets your body moving the way we want before the intent goes up.

Long toss and pulldowns

Long toss climbs from 60% to 85% and up as the weeks go on. Pulldowns come in once you've earned them.

Then the mound

Velo has to show up where it counts. Mound prep and mound throws lead into a velocity-focused bullpen.

Lifts and jumps

Lower body strength, then power, plus jumps, upper body and core, so the weight room is working for the throwing.

Eyes on your work

FaceTime me in, or send the video after. Arm care and recovery days are in the plan too, not an afterthought.
